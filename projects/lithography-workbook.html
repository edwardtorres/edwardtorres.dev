Technical details

Chapter and field definitions are data-driven in vanilla JavaScript. Answers remain in localStorage, completion is derived from required fields, and the printable report is assembled entirely in the browser without an application backend.

Safety, provenance & limitations

This is a recording aid for the named ExSTENT/Nano3 learning activity—not an official SOP, equipment authorization, or chemical-safety reference. Current facility procedures, SDS documents, PPE requirements, equipment rules, and instructor guidance always override the application.

Screenshot values are synthetic. Browser-local data can be lost when site storage is cleared, and externally sourced instructional content still requires permission and provenance review before broader reuse.
